Capital receipt chain

From request to close packet.

This is not another finance screen. It is a memory system for the awkward middle: request, approval, money movement, match, exception, close.

Invoice, counterparty, bank reference, entity, and purpose arrive as one packet instead of five loose tabs.

02

03

Money matches
