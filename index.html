Most firms run their money, their movement, and their people on different systems. We don't think they should.

Three verticals.
One system.

Pick one. Pick all three. The goal is the same ledger, the same access model, and the same command surface, so new workflows can be added without starting from scratch.

01
Finance
Capital operations

Payment routing, reconciliation workflow, and cash-position review. Designed to reduce handoffs between accounting, bank portals, and controller spreadsheets.

  • Routing rules for wires, ACH, batches, amounts, and counterparties
  • Bank-connected reconciliation workflow for supported institutions
  • Multi-currency ledger model with position review
  • Exportable event history for finance and tax review
02
Freight
Physical operations

Dispatch, fleet asset tracking, and vendor scorecards connected to the same operating ledger. For brokers, carriers, and 3PLs coordinating mid-market volume.

  • Dispatch board structure for GPS, ETA, and detention alerts
  • Asset tracker with maintenance scheduling
  • Vendor scorecard: on-time, margin, incident history
  • Lane analytics and cost-per-mile optimization
03
Consulting
Engagement management

Scope, milestone, and pay specialist engagements end-to-end. Payment workflow is designed to stay connected to finance controls instead of running around close.

  • Milestone-gated approval and payout workflow
  • Specialist roster and engagement matching structure
  • Document bundle for NDA, W-9, and client requirements
  • Engagement boards with NPS and re-engagement scoring
