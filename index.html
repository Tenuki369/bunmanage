FAQ

Common questions.

Can I really get started in under 5 minutes?
Yes. The intake flow takes ~3 minutes, your workspace is provisioned instantly, and connecting your first bank account or integration takes another 2 minutes. There's no sales call, no implementation consultant, and no spreadsheet hand-off. You can start routing real transactions on day one.
Do I have to use all four pillars?
No. Start with one pillar and add others when you're ready. All four share the same ledger, RBAC, and Cmd+K surface — so adding a pillar means your data is already there, not in a new silo. Most teams start with Capital or Ops and add Talent and Cloud within 90 days.
What does "volume-based pricing" mean exactly?
You pay a flat monthly rate with a volume cap included. Starter includes up to $5M/month routed through Capital. Growth includes up to $50M/month. Atlas is unlimited. There are no per-seat fees, no per-transaction fees, and no surprise overages — volume tiers just adjust your monthly rate automatically if you move up.
How does migration from McLeod or QuickBooks work?
We have native import tools for QuickBooks exports and McLeod data packs. Most Capital migrations complete in one weekend — we import historical transactions, map your chart of accounts, and reconcile the opening position before you go live. Growth and Atlas tiers include a dedicated migration session with our onboarding team.
Is my data auditable and exportable?
Yes. The BunManage ledger is immutable — every event is timestamped, actor-stamped, and cryptographically hashed. You can export your full ledger at any time in CSV, JSON, or directly to your Postgres schema. SOC 2 Type II audit reports are available on request for all tiers.

Quick start

Get from zero to your first routed fund transaction in under 5 minutes. No sales call. The intake flow provisions your workspace automatically. No sales call required. The intake flow provisions your workspace automatically.

BunManage by Bains Fund Management LLC is currently in private beta. Complete the 4-question intake → to get early access. Most workspaces go live within minutes of completing intake.

1. Complete intake

The intake flow asks 4 questions about your business: what you move, your monthly volume, your primary pain point, and what matters most right now. It takes under 3 minutes.

Based on your answers, we provision a workspace with sensible defaults — routing rule templates, RBAC roles, and integration suggestions pre-loaded.

2. Connect your first integration

For Capital: connect your bank account via Plaid or enter your Chase Business credentials directly. For Ops: connect your ELD provider or Samsara fleet API. All connections use OAuth or API key — no custom code required.

# Install the BunManage CLI (optional) npm install -g @bunmanage/cli # or: pip install bunmanage # Authenticate bunmanage auth login # List your workspace pillars bunmanage pillars list # Trigger your first routing rule test bunmanage capital route --amount 50000 --vendor chase --dry-run

3. Invite your team

Add team members from Settings → Team. RBAC roles are pre-seeded based on your intake answers:

POST /api/v1/workspace/members Content-Type: application/json Authorization: Bearer {your_api_key} { "email": "ops-lead@yourcompany.com", "role": "ops_manager", "pillars": ["capital", "ops"] }
